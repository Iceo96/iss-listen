Our (friendly, legal) target

The ISS on 145.800 MHz

Why it's perfect

  • Strong FM downlink, 400 km up, passes overhead daily
  • Unencrypted & unauthenticated — like most legacy RF
  • Receiving is 100% legal & passive
  • Carries voice (crew/ARISS), SSTV images, APRS packet

The catch: it's moving at 7.66 km/s

The signal gets smeared by ±3.5 kHz of Doppler shift — the same effect as a passing ambulance siren dropping in pitch.

Ignore it → the audio distorts and fades. This is the problem we solve.

Under the hood

How it works

One command ties it all together: iss-listen.

The engineering flex

Gapless Doppler — in software

The obvious fix: keep re-tuning the radio to chase the Doppler.

We measured it on the RTL-SDR v4:

~950 ms

of dead air per re-tune. Unacceptable.

So we never re-tune the hardware. We park on one frequency and cancel the Doppler in software → zero gaps.

The DSP chain

IQ ──▶ NCO shift (−Δf)
──▶ low-pass + ÷16
──▶ FM discriminator
──▶ 🔊 15 kHz audio

A numerically-controlled oscillator spins the moving carrier back to 0 Hz, 4× per second, from the live SGP4 range-rate.

Knowing where to look

Orbital prediction = open OSINT

  • Every tracked object's orbit is public data (TLEs from Celestrak).
  • SGP4 + skyfield turn two lines of text into exact rise / peak / set times for your location.
  • Cached locally, refreshed every 3 days — works offline after that.
  • Recon before reception — the same first step in any signals-intelligence workflow.

Why this matters to OWASP

The RF attack surface

Receive

ADS-B aircraft, AIS ships, APRS, pagers, 433 MHz sensors, old GSM — plaintext, spoofable, unauthenticated.

Transmit (⚠️ illegal)

GPS/GNSS spoofing, ADS-B injection, satellite hijacking (Viasat 2022, DEF CON sat talks).

The lesson

If it's wireless and old, assume no confidentiality, no authenticity. Design accordingly.

🧭 Ethics: our tool only listens. Receiving is legal and passive. The moment you transmit without a licence, you're breaking the law — and that line matters.
